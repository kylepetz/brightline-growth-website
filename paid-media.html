What we usually find

Most ad accounts we review have the same three problems.

  • Campaigns optimized for cheap clicks
  • Tracking that counts the wrong events
  • Budgets spread across too many tests to learn anything

We fix those first, then grow revenue at a cost you can afford.

How an engagement works

  1. 1

    Audit

    We review your accounts and results, then send a written summary of what to change first.

  2. 2

    Tracking check

    We confirm conversions are recorded correctly before touching bids or budgets.

  3. 3

    Restructure

    We rebuild campaigns where needed and launch the first creative tests.

  4. 4

    Manage

    We check accounts several times a week, shift budget, and add new ads.

  5. 5

    Report

    Slack or email, a live dashboard, and regular calls, set up around how your team works.

What we report on

Numbers that show whether your ad spend is paying off.

  • Cost per customer
  • Return on ad spend
  • Blended CAC
  • Payback period

Clicks, impressions, and click-through rate help us diagnose problems. They don't tell you whether ads are paying off, so they aren't the headline.

How we use AI

We're an AI-forward consultancy. AI handles the routine work, so more of our time goes to strategy, creative testing, and your results.

  • Checks accounts for problems like broken links or budgets spending too fast
  • Summarizes performance and drafts reports
  • Handles routine edits across campaigns

A person reviews AI output before anyone acts on it.
