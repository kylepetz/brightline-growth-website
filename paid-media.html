Services / Paid media

Paid media management

We set up, restructure, and manage Google, Meta, Microsoft, and LinkedIn ad accounts, and we judge every decision by cost per customer and revenue.

Most ad accounts we review have the same problems: campaigns optimized for cheap clicks, tracking that counts the wrong events, and budgets spread across too many tests to learn anything. We fix those problems first, then focus on growing revenue at a cost you can afford.

What's included

We take over day-to-day management of your ad accounts. Depending on where your customers are, that covers some or all of the following:

  • Google Ads: Search, Shopping, Performance Max, and YouTube, including keyword research, negative keyword lists, bid strategy, and product feed checks.
  • Meta: Facebook and Instagram prospecting and retargeting, audience setup, and Advantage+ campaigns where they make sense.
  • Microsoft Ads: Bing search and shopping campaigns, usually imported from Google and then tuned for their own costs and audiences.
  • LinkedIn Ads: sponsored content and lead gen forms for businesses that sell to specific job titles or industries.
  • Account structure: rebuilding campaigns so each one has a clear job and enough budget to learn.
  • Creative testing: a regular schedule of new ads, judged by cost per customer rather than click-through rate.
  • Budget allocation: moving spend toward the campaigns and channels that bring in profitable customers.

Who it's for

We work with DTC ecommerce brands, subscription businesses, and lead generation companies across the US and Canada. You'll get the most from this service if people already buy your product, your website converts, and you want paid media to grow at a cost you can predict.

It's also a good fit if you've worked with an agency before and couldn't tell which results were real. Your ad accounts and data stay in your name. You keep full ownership and admin access at all times, and we explain every change we make.

How an engagement works

  1. Audit. We review your ad accounts, tracking, and recent results, then send a written summary of what's working, what isn't, and what we'd change first.
  2. Tracking check. Before we change bids or budgets, we confirm conversions are recorded correctly. If they aren't, we fix that first.
  3. Restructure. We rebuild campaigns where needed, set budgets, and launch the first round of creative tests.
  4. Ongoing management. We check your accounts several times a week, shift budget, add new ads, and turn off what isn't working.
  5. Reporting and check-ins. We set this up around how your team works: regular contact over Slack or email, a reporting dashboard you can open any time, and scheduled calls to review results and plan the next tests.

What we report on

We report on cost per customer, return on ad spend, blended customer acquisition cost, and payback period. Clicks, impressions, and click-through rate help diagnose problems, but they don't tell you whether your ad spend is paying off, so they aren't the focus of our reports.

How we use AI

We're an AI-forward consultancy. We use AI tools throughout our work to check accounts for problems, summarize performance, draft reports, and handle routine edits across campaigns. That saves hours each week and helps make sure small details, like a broken link or a budget running too fast, get caught early. The time we save goes into strategy, creative testing, and your results. A person reviews everything before it reaches your accounts.

Pricing

Engagements start at $2,500 per month for retainers and $1,000 for projects, such as a one-time account audit. Retainers have a 3-month minimum, then continue month to month. After a first call, we send a short written plan with pricing for your accounts.

Who you'll work with

Your accounts are managed by a senior marketer with more than 15 years in growth marketing, working with national DTC and subscription brands. The person you meet with is the person doing the work, with AI tools handling the routine parts.
