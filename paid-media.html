Paid media management

I set up, clean up, and run ad accounts, and I judge every decision by cost per customer and revenue.

  • Google Ads
  • Meta
  • Microsoft Ads
  • LinkedIn Ads

What I usually find

Most accounts I look at have some mix of these:

  • Campaigns optimized for cheap clicks
  • Tracking that counts the wrong things
  • Budget spread across too many tests to learn anything

I fix those first. Then it's about growing revenue at a cost per customer that works for you.

What's included

I run your accounts day to day on the platforms where your customers are.

  • Google Ads

    Search, Shopping, Performance Max, and YouTube.

  • Meta

    Facebook and Instagram prospecting, retargeting, and Advantage+.

  • Microsoft Ads

    Bing search and shopping. Usually a copy of Google to start, then tuned on its own.

  • LinkedIn Ads

    Sponsored content and lead gen forms when you're going after specific roles or industries.

  • Account structure

How it works

  1. 1

    Audit

    I go through your accounts and results, then send a written summary of what I'd change first.

  2. 2

    Tracking check

    Before touching bids or budgets, I make sure conversions are being counted right.

  3. 3

    Restructure

    I rebuild campaigns where needed and get the first creative tests live.

  4. 4

    Manage

    I'm in your accounts several times a week shifting budget, adding ads, and cutting what isn't working.

  5. 5

    Report

    Slack or email, a live dashboard, and regular calls. Whatever works for your team.

What I report on

The numbers that tell you if your ads are paying off.

  • Cost per customer
  • Return on ad spend
  • Blended CAC
  • Payback period

Clicks, impressions, and click-through rate are useful for figuring out what's wrong. They don't tell you if you're making money, so they aren't the headline.

How I use AI

I'm AI-forward in how I work. AI handles a lot of the routine work, which frees me up for strategy and testing and catches details I'd otherwise have to go hunting for.

  • Checking accounts for problems like broken links or budgets spending too fast
  • Summarizing performance and drafting reports
  • Routine edits across campaigns

I review anything AI produces before it goes live.
