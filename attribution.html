Marketing attribution and tracking

Tracking and reporting that matches your actual revenue, so you can see which channels bring in customers.

  • GA4
  • Google Tag Manager
  • Looker Studio
  • Google Sheets
  • Shopify

The problem

Every ad platform takes credit for as many sales as it can. Add up what each one reports, and you often get more conversions than you actually had.

Our attribution work gives you one set of numbers you can trust and plan around.

What's included

Tracking you can trust, and reporting built on your own sales data.

  • Tracking audit

    Pixels, tags, and events checked across the ad platforms, GA4, and Tag Manager.

  • Conversion APIs

    Meta Conversions API and Google enhanced conversions, so sales count when browsers block pixels.

  • Offline conversions

    Sales, qualified leads, and calls sent from your CRM back to the ad platforms.

  • Event cleanup

    One consistent set of events, with each purchase and lead counted once.

  • UTM and naming standards

    A simple system for tagging links and naming campaigns, so reports group correctly.

  • Revenue reporting

    Blended CAC, MER, ROAS, and payback period from Shopify or your CRM.

  • Dashboards

    Looker Studio or Google Sheets, connected to your own data and shared with your team.

  • Ongoing monitoring

    Tracking breaks when sites change. We watch for it and fix it.

Who it's for

  • You spend on more than one ad channel
  • Your Meta, Google, and Shopify numbers don't agree
  • You recently rebuilt your site, changed platforms, or switched agencies

For DTC ecommerce, subscription, and lead generation businesses. Attribution is part of our paid media management, inside the retainer or as an added project.

You own everything. Your ad accounts, tracking, and data stay in your name, with full admin access at all times. We explain every change we make.

How an engagement works

  1. 1

    Discovery call

    We learn how you sell, which tools you use, and which numbers your team relies on.

  2. 2

    Audit

    We test tracking end to end, compare platform numbers to real sales, and rank every issue by impact.

  3. 3

    Fix and set up

    We correct tags and events, add conversion APIs and offline conversions, and document each change.

  4. 4

    Build reporting

    We build a dashboard of spend, customers, and revenue by channel, and walk your team through it.

  5. 5

    Monitor

    We keep watching tracking as part of the retainer and update you over Slack, email, and calls.

Our view on attribution models

No model is perfect. Platforms over-count, last-click under-credits the channels that start the buying process, and privacy changes keep shrinking what can be tracked.

Our baseline

Blended numbers like MER and total CAC. We use platform and analytics data to decide where to move budget within them.
