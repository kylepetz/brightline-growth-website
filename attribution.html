Services / Attribution

Marketing attribution and tracking

We set up tracking and reporting that matches your actual revenue, so you can see which channels and campaigns are bringing in customers.

Every ad platform takes credit for as many sales as it can. Add up what Google, Meta, and Microsoft report and you often get more conversions than you actually had. Our attribution work gives you one set of numbers you can trust and plan around.

What's included

  • Tracking audit: we check your pixels, tags, and conversion events in Google Ads, Meta, Microsoft Ads, LinkedIn, Google Analytics 4, and Google Tag Manager, and list every gap and duplicate we find.
  • Conversion APIs: server-side tracking with the Meta Conversions API and Google enhanced conversions, so sales are still recorded when browsers block pixels.
  • Offline conversions: sending sales, qualified leads, and calls from your CRM back to the ad platforms, so campaigns optimize for real revenue.
  • Event cleanup: one consistent set of events and values across platforms, with each purchase and lead counted once.
  • UTM and naming standards: a simple system for tagging links and naming campaigns so reports group correctly.
  • Revenue reporting: blended CAC, marketing efficiency ratio (MER), return on ad spend, and payback period, calculated from Shopify analytics or your CRM rather than platform estimates.
  • Dashboards: reports in Looker Studio or Google Sheets, connected to your own data and shared with your team.

Who it's for

This is for DTC ecommerce, subscription, and lead generation businesses that spend on more than one ad channel and can't get a straight answer to a simple question: which ad spend is producing customers? It's common when the numbers in Meta, Google, and Shopify don't agree, or after a site rebuild, a platform change, or a switch between agencies.

Attribution is offered as part of our paid media management, either inside the monthly retainer or as an added project. When we manage your ads, this work comes first. Your tracking accounts and data stay in your name, and you keep full ownership and admin access.

How an engagement works

  1. Discovery call. We learn how you sell, which tools you use, and which numbers your team relies on.
  2. Audit. We test your tracking end to end, place test orders or leads where possible, and compare platform numbers to your actual sales. You get a written list of issues ranked by impact.
  3. Fixes and setup. We correct tags and events, set up conversion APIs and offline conversions, and document every change.
  4. Reporting build. We build a dashboard that shows spend, customers, and revenue by channel, and walk your team through it.
  5. Ongoing checks. Tracking breaks when sites change. We monitor it as part of the retainer and keep you updated over Slack or email and on regular calls.

A note on attribution models

No attribution model is perfect. Platform numbers over-count, last-click analytics under-credits the channels that start the buying process, and privacy changes keep reducing what can be tracked. We use blended numbers like MER and total CAC as the baseline, then use platform and analytics data to decide where to move budget within that.

We've also worked with multi-touch attribution tools such as Triple Whale and Northbeam, marketing mix modeling platforms such as Measured, and holdout and geographic lift tests. You don't need any of them to start. If your spend and channel mix reach the point where one would pay for itself, we'll recommend it and help you set it up.

How we use AI

We're an AI-forward consultancy. We use AI tools to compare numbers across platforms, flag tracking that stops firing, and draft the summaries that go with each report. That saves time on manual checks and helps make sure no detail gets missed when there are dozens of tags and events to keep track of. A person reviews every finding before we act on it.
