Information we collect

We collect only what you give us and the minimum a website needs to run.

Information you provide

When you use our contact form or email us, we receive your name, email address, and anything else you choose to include, such as your company, website, ad spend range and message.

Information collected automatically

This site does not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies. Like any website, the services that host and deliver it may log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and discuss a potential engagement.
  • To provide services if you become a client, under a separate written agreement.
  • To keep records required for legal, tax or accounting purposes.
  • To protect the site and our business from fraud and abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or add you to a marketing list without your consent.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run this site and our business, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • FormSubmit delivers contact form submissions to our inbox.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on this site, which means your browser requests font files from Google.
  • Our email provider, which stores correspondence.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our terms, or in connection with a sale or reorganization of the business.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and follow up, generally no more than 24 months if no engagement results, unless the law requires longer. Client records are kept for the period our agreements and tax obligations require.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing hello@brightlinegrowth.com.

California residents

If the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the CPRA, applies to us, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to request deletion or correction; to opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither); to limit use of sensitive personal information (we don't collect it); and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We will verify requests by confirming control of the email address involved.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS and we use reasonable safeguards to protect information we receive. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please don't send passwords, account numbers or other sensitive data through the contact form.

Children

This site is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices change. The "last updated" date above shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be posted on this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email hello@brightlinegrowth.com or use our contact form.
