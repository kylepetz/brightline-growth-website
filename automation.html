Services / Automation

Marketing automation

We build lifecycle flows, CRM automation, product feed rules, and AI-assisted reporting so campaigns, data, and follow-up run without constant manual work.

Many marketing teams spend hours each week on work a system could handle: copying numbers into reports, fixing product feeds, and following up with leads by hand. We set up automation that handles those tasks reliably, and we document it so your team can maintain it.

What's included

  • Lifecycle email and SMS: welcome series, abandoned cart and browse flows, post-purchase follow-up, and win-back campaigns in Klaviyo, Attentive, or Braze.
  • CRM automation: lead routing, lead scoring, deal stage updates, and alerts for your sales team in HubSpot or GoHighLevel.
  • Connections between tools: Zapier workflows that move data between your store, CRM, and ad platforms without manual exports.
  • Product feed management: cleaning and enriching the product feeds that Shopify sends to Google Merchant Center, Microsoft, and Meta, with rules that fix titles, categories, and missing fields automatically.
  • Conversion syncing: sending CRM stages, purchases, and customer lists back to the ad platforms so campaigns optimize for real outcomes.
  • AI-assisted reporting: automatic performance summaries and alerts when spend, costs, or results move outside their normal range.
  • Ad operations tooling: custom scripts for bulk edits, feed fixes, and routine account checks.
  • Documentation: a written guide for every automation, covering what it does, what triggers it, and how to change it.

Who it's for

This fits DTC ecommerce, subscription, and lead generation businesses with a growing customer list and a small team. Common signs: leads wait too long for a reply, product feeds get disapproved, monthly reports take a day to put together, or your email platform only sends a newsletter.

Automation is offered as part of our paid media management, either inside the monthly retainer or as an added project. Sending CRM and purchase data back to Google and Meta helps their algorithms find buyers rather than browsers, so the two work best together. Everything we build lives in your accounts, and you keep full ownership of it and your data.

How an engagement works

  1. Review. We map your current tools, how data moves between them, and the manual tasks your team repeats each week.
  2. Plan. We send a prioritized list of automations, starting with the ones that save the most time or recover the most revenue.
  3. Build and test. We build each automation in your accounts, test it with real data, and check unusual cases before turning it on.
  4. Handoff. We walk your team through how each automation works and share the documentation.
  5. Ongoing support. We monitor automations, fix anything that breaks, and add new flows as your business changes, with regular contact over Slack or email and scheduled calls.

How we use AI

We're an AI-forward consultancy, and automation is where that shows most. We use AI in our own tooling, reporting, and account operations to get more done in less time and to make sure the details are covered. We bring the same approach to your business, so your team spends less time on routine work and more on decisions that need a person.

We automate repetitive, rules-based work. Decisions about budgets, pricing, and brand messaging still get a person's sign-off, and a person reviews AI output before anyone acts on it.
