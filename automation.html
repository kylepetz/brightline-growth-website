What's included

Automation across your email, CRM, store, and ad platforms.

  • Lifecycle email and SMS

    Welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, and win-back flows.

  • CRM automation

    Lead routing, lead scoring, deal stage updates, and sales alerts.

  • Tool connections

    Zapier workflows that move data between tools without manual exports.

  • Product feeds

    Rules that fix titles, categories, and missing fields in feeds to Google, Microsoft, and Meta.

  • Conversion syncing

    CRM stages and purchases sent back to the ad platforms, so campaigns optimize for real outcomes.

  • AI-assisted reporting

    Automatic summaries, plus alerts when results move outside their normal range.

  • Ad operations tooling

    Custom scripts for bulk edits, feed fixes, and routine account checks.

  • Documentation

    A written guide for every automation: what it does, what triggers it, and how to change it.
